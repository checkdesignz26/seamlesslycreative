Hi, I'm the person behind CheckDesignz — pattern designer, creative tinkerer, and someone who apparently can't stop turning little ideas into apps. 😂

I've been creating patterns for around eight years, starting out with Illustrator before moving through Affinity Designer, Procreate and other creative tools. Along the way, I discovered something I love almost as much as making patterns: finding a better, easier way to do things.

And that's really how all of this started.

I'd be working on a pattern and think, "There has to be an easier way to do this…" — and instead of just accepting it, I started building little tools to solve the problem.

One idea became another, and another, and suddenly I had a whole collection of creative apps.

That's how CheckDesignz grew into what it is today — a little creative studio filled with tools I actually wanted to use myself.

From creating seamless patterns and collections, to resizing artwork, making mock-ups and putting together Etsy listings, my aim is simple:

Make the fiddly bits easier, so you can get back to the fun part — creating.

I'm not a big software company. It's just me, my patterns, a ridiculous number of ideas, and a tendency to say "Ooooh… what if the app could do this?" 😂

And there are still plenty more ideas brewing.

Welcome to my little corner of the creative world. 💛

Make something lovely. Make it easier. And most importantly — have fun making it.
